POSTAR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

POSTAR is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Vice Clips to upload and schedule their own YouTube Shorts. It has no other users.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth, with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, POSTAR only uploads videos to the owner's own channel and reads the channel name to confirm it is the right account. It does not access, collect or store data from any other user or channel.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to third parties, sold or shared. No cookies, analytics or ads are used.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. All stored data is deleted by deleting the local token file. POSTAR keeps no copy of any YouTube data.

YouTube API Services

POSTAR uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
