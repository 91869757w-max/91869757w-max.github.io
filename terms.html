POSTAR is a private desktop tool, not offered to the public, used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Vice Clips to upload and schedule their own YouTube Shorts.

POSTAR uses YouTube API Services. By using POSTAR you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

Only the channel owner. POSTAR has no sign-up, no public service and no other users.

It uploads videos created and approved by the owner to the owner's own channel (at most one per day) and reads the connected channel name to confirm the account. It does not read, modify or delete data from any other channel.
